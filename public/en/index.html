Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to set up an automation?
It depends on the complexity, but an average process takes between two weeks and a month. We start with quick mapping, and within a few days, you'll see the first automations running.

Is it suitable for small businesses too?
Absolutely. Especially in small businesses where every minute counts and there is no spare manpower, automation is a critical "force multiplier" that allows you to grow without hiring more staff.

How much does it cost?
Every project is different, so we don't work with off-the-shelf packages. After the initial mapping call (which is free), we provide a detailed and transparent quote based on your exact needs — no surprises.

Will I need to know how to code?
Not at all. All the systems we build are No-Code or Low-Code. At the end of the process, you'll receive training that will allow you to understand exactly what's happening "under the hood" without writing a single line of code.

Do you work with existing CRM systems?
Yes, we specialize in connecting and maximizing the capabilities of systems like Monday, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and more. If your system is open to connections (API), we know how to automate it.
